Refund & return policy

Every Pulse business app comes with a free trial, so you can test everything before you pay. If a paid licence doesn't work and we can't fix it, we give your money back.

Applies to: EduPulse, PulseHMIS, PulseRemit Pro, PulseStock and PulseProperty Pro, sold by Pulse Generation UG, Uganda. Last updated 23 October 2026.

1. Try before you pay

  • Each business app has a free 30-day trial with every feature switched on. No payment or card is needed to start.
  • Please use the trial to check the app suits your organisation before you buy a licence.
  • Lwasa Ludo is free, so there is nothing to pay and nothing to refund.

2. When you get a full refund

We refund the full amount you paid if you ask within 7 days of payment and:

  • the licence key we sent you does not activate the app, or
  • the app has a fault that stops you using it, and our support team cannot fix it within 7 days of your report, or
  • you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount by mistake (we refund the extra amount at any time).

Software is delivered digitally as a licence key, so a "return" means we deactivate that licence key when we refund you.

3. When refunds are not given

  • Requests made more than 7 days after payment.
  • A change of mind after the licence has been used for real work.
  • Unused days or months of a subscription you cancel early. Your licence keeps working until it expires.
  • Problems caused by your own hardware, network or internet provider, or by changes made to the app or its data by someone other than us.

4. Exchanges

Within 7 days of payment you can switch your licence to a different plan or a different Pulse app. We move what you paid to the new licence, and you pay or receive any difference in price.
