EduPulse questions

Will updates ever erase our data?{{icon:plus}}

No. EduPulse upgrades its database step by step with migrations and never replaces it, so your records carry forward through every update.

How do backups work?{{icon:plus}}

Back up manually or on a schedule, as a database file or a ZIP that includes photos and documents, to Documents, the desktop, an external drive or USB. Before a restore, EduPulse confirms the administrator and takes an automatic safety backup.

Can we use terms or semesters?{{icon:plus}}

Either. Choose Term 1–3 or Semester 1–2 per institution; nothing is hard-coded.

Can we bring in our existing student list?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Import from CSV or Excel: select the file, preview, map columns and validate. Errors are shown before anything is saved.

Can you help set it up at our school?{{icon:plus}}

Absolutely. Request a demo or message us on WhatsApp.
