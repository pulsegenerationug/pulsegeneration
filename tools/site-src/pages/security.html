Security & your data

Patient files, exam marks, savings balances and rent ledgers are sensitive. This page explains, in plain words, where that data lives and how our apps protect it.

{{icon:devices}}

Records live on your devices

Patients, students, accounts and stock are kept in a database on your own main computer and shared over your own local network. Day-to-day work needs no internet.

{{icon:shield}}

Passwords are never stored

Only a salted, hashed form of each password is kept, so nobody, including us, can read a user's password from the database.

{{icon:person}}

Everyone sees only their part

Each user has a role. A cashier, a teacher or a pharmacist sees and changes only what their role allows.

{{icon:flag}}

An audit trail

Important actions are recorded with who did them and when, so managers can check what happened.

{{icon:share}}

Backups you control

Make a backup from Settings and keep a copy somewhere safe, such as an external drive, Google Drive or another computer. Restore it on any device.

{{icon:wifi-off}}

Offline keeps working

A dropped connection never stops work. Online features simply catch up when the internet returns.

When the apps go online

Only the main computer talks to the Pulse cloud, and only when it has internet. Other devices go through it. It is used for:

  • Your licence. The main computer registers your organisation, reports its licence status and receives licence keys, which activate by themselves.
  • Branches. Sites in other towns join with a one-time code. Messages between head office and branches travel through the cloud; messages inside one site stay on your network.
  • Messages from Pulse Generation UG, such as update notices.
  • PulseLMIS also reports user and activity counts, not results or patient details.
  • PulseRemit Pro shares account balances, the loan book and the receipt register between your own branches, so any branch can check an account or verify a receipt. Its off-site backups are encrypted on your computer first, and the backup password never leaves it.

Each organisation's cloud data is kept apart, and every main computer proves who it is with its own secret key.

On the local network, PulseRemit Pro, PulseStock and PulseProperty Pro also encrypt every request between devices (AES-GCM).

Good habits we recommend

  • Give every member of staff their own login, and remove it when they leave.
  • Change the default administrator password on the first day.
  • Back up every day, and keep at least one copy away from the building.
  • Lock computers when you step away, and keep Windows and Android up to date.
  • Download Pulse apps only from this website.
