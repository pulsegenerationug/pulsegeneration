Pricing questions

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?{{icon:plus}}

The app asks for a licence. Your data stays where it is: buy a licence and carry on exactly where you stopped.

How do I pay?{{icon:plus}}

Inside the app, choose a plan and pay with MTN or Airtel Mobile Money. The licence key arrives and activates by itself. You can also pay us directly and we send the key.

Do I pay per computer or per user?{{icon:plus}}

No. One licence covers your organisation: every computer, phone and user on your network, and every branch that joins.

Can I change plan or app later?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Within 7 days of payment you can switch plan or app and we move what you paid. See the refund & return policy.

What does "Lifetime" mean?{{icon:plus}}

One payment and the licence never expires.
