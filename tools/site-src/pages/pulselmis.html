PulseLMIS questions

Does it need internet?{{icon:plus}}

No. One computer is the Host; other computers, tablets and phones on the same network connect to it, and analysers send results to it. Internet is only used, when available, for licence delivery, branches, staff messages and support.

Do patient results go to the cloud?{{icon:plus}}

No. Patients, results, reports and invoices stay on your computers. The cloud only carries the licence, user names, activity counts, staff messages between branches and the support chat.

Can it print on our letterhead?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Upload your logos, set the header, footer and signature in Settings › Branding & printing, choose the paper for each document and use Test print. Pre-printed stationery is supported too.

Which analysers does it connect to?{{icon:plus}}

Any analyser that speaks ASTM (LIS1-A / LIS2-A2) or HL7 over the network or a shared folder. Ready code maps exist for common Sysmex, Mindray, Roche, Beckman Coulter, Siemens, Abbott, Stago and HbA1c analysers.

How do we pay for the licence?{{icon:plus}}

MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. Send the transaction ID from Settings › Subscription; the key arrives in the app by itself, or on WhatsApp for offline activation.
