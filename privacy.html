Privacy

How Pulse Generation UG handles information submitted through this website.

What information is collected

This website collects only the information you choose to submit through the contact form, the demo request form, or the interest registration form — for example your name, email address, phone number, organization, and message. The website itself does not collect patient health data or financial account data; those are handled inside the PulseHMIS and FixIt Enterprise applications themselves, separately from this website.

Why information is collected

Information submitted through these forms is used solely to respond to your inquiry, demo request, or interest registration — for example, to contact you about PulseHMIS, FixIt Enterprise, or a support question.

How requests are handled

Form submissions are reviewed by Pulse Generation UG and used only for the purpose you submitted them for. Submitted information is never sold, and it is not exposed publicly.

Data & account deletion

If you would like any information you've submitted to be deleted, email pulsegenerationug@gmail.com or lwasapulse11@gmail.com with your request, and it will be actioned.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pulsegenerationug@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
