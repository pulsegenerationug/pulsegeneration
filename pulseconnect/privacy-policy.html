PulseConnect - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 (version 1.4.1)

PulseConnect is an app for chatting, calling, finding service providers and buying and selling, made by Pulse Generation UG, Kampala, Uganda ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

1. What stays on your device

Your chats, photos, videos, voice notes, call history, contacts you talk to and drafts are kept on your own device, in a database that is encrypted with a key held by your phone or computer's secure keystore. Uninstalling the app removes them unless you made a backup.

2. What we keep on our servers

  • Account: your e-mail address and password (handled by our sign-in service; we never see the password itself), and your phone number so friends can find you.
  • Public profile: your name, account type (customer, service provider or seller), business name, category, city, country, description, profile photo and the social media links you add. Other users can see these.
  • Messages on their way: a message, photo or file you send waits on our server only until the other person's device receives it, and is then deleted. Undelivered items are deleted after 180 days. Group messages stay available to the group's members while they are in the group.
  • Groups: group name, photo, description, members and their roles, and which messages each member has received and read (for ticks).
  • Calls: the short technical messages needed to connect a call (no audio or video is recorded or stored).
  • Feed, listings, reviews and reports: posts, products and services you publish, reviews you write, and reports you send about abuse.
  • Notifications: a push token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can tell your device about new messages and calls, and your notification settings.
  • Subscriptions: for service providers and sellers, the state of your subscription. Payments are made directly to Pulse Generation UG; we do not store card numbers.

3. Identity check (optional)

Providers and sellers may verify their identity. The ID document and selfie video are checked on your device. Only the result, a one-way fingerprint of the document number and, if a person must review it, the photos are sent to us. Review photos are deleted as soon as the decision is made.

4. Backups

Backups are encrypted with your own passphrase before they leave your device (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305). If you choose Google Drive backup, the encrypted file is saved in your own Google Drive; we cannot open it, and without your passphrase or 24-word recovery code nobody can.

5. Crash reports and usage statistics

If the app crashes, a technical error report (without your messages or contacts) is sent to us so we can fix it. Anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens are opened) are sent only if you leave "Share usage statistics" on in Settings; you can turn it off at any time.

6. Who we share with

We use these service providers only to run the app: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and, if you choose it, Google Drive (your backup). We may disclose information if the law of Uganda requires it. We never share your information with advertisers.

7. Permissions

Camera and microphone (calls, photos, videos, voice notes and the identity check), photos and files (to send attachments and save media you choose), notifications, and the network. Each is used only when you use that feature.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and social links at any time in the app.
  • Block or report anyone; blocked people cannot message you or add you to groups.
  • Delete your account in the app (Me › Account › Delete account) or as described on pulsegenerationug.com/delete-account. This deletes your login, profile, posts, listings, reviews, identity records, push tokens and files from our servers.
  • You may ask for a copy or correction of your information under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

9. Children

PulseConnect is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted (HTTPS), data on your device is encrypted, and our servers only let each person read what they are allowed to read. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you notice a problem.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses information, and show the date at the top.
