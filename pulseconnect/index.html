Questions

Someone sent me a group invite link

Install PulseConnect, sign in, then tap the link again. You can also open the app, tap the pencil, choose "Join with a link" and paste it.

Updating Android from 1.4.0 or older

From 1.4.1 PulseConnect is signed with the Pulse Generation UG release key. Back up in Me › Backup first, uninstall the old app, install the new one and restore.

Deleting my account

In the app: Me › Account › Delete account. Or see how to delete your account.
