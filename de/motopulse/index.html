MotoPulse questions

Is it for a car wash, a parking or a garage?

All three, and boda wash points. At setup choose what you do; MotoPulse fills in starting services, prices per vehicle type and parking rates that you can change at any time.

A customer says “I will pay you tomorrow”. What happens?

Choose Pay later: MotoPulse records the amount, the promised date, a note and who allowed it. It shows on the customer’s profile and on the debtors list until it is paid, and a payment always clears the oldest bill first. Walk-ins without a profile always pay in full.

How do daily customers work?

Register them once with their daily amount. Each day one tap records their arrival, parks the vehicle if you want and charges the day once — paid now or left on the account. You see who came, who is parked and who has paid.

Braucht es Internet?

No. One computer is the Host; phones and tablets at the gate connect to it on the same WiFi. Internet is used, when available, for branch totals, licence delivery and support messages.
