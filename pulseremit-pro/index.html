Banking & SACCO services

Savings, deposits and withdrawals, done right.

One Banking hub puts every counter job one tap away, on a Windows PC at the counter or an Android tablet in the field.

  • Open accounts. Savings, current and fixed-deposit products with check-digit account numbers and manager approval.
  • Deposits & withdrawals. Photo and signature check on every withdrawal, holds, limits and approval above your threshold.
  • Check balances. Balance enquiry with a printable mini statement; any branch can look up an account opened at another branch.

Branch network

Counters connect in seconds.

One computer is the branch Host. Counter PCs and tablets find it on the local network and join with the Host's PIN, or by scanning its QR code. Head office and other towns connect through the encrypted Pulse cloud.

  • Works without internet inside the branch; syncs when a connection returns.
  • Every bank is isolated. Per-computer keys and locked-down cloud functions: one bank can never see another's data.
  • Encrypted cloud backups locked with the bank's own password.

PulseRemit Pro questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Each branch runs on its own Host computer and local network. The internet is only needed to connect branches and head office, and they catch up automatically when it returns.

Can another bank see our data?

No. Every computer has its own secret key, the cloud only returns your own bank's records, and cloud backups are encrypted with your password before they leave your computer.

How do tablets connect to the branch?

Open Connect on the tablet: it finds the Host on the network. Type the Host PIN (letters and numbers) or simply scan the QR code shown on the Host's Devices screen.

Do we pay for every computer?

No. Only your head-office computer is licensed. Branch, counter and tablet devices are free.

Can you set it up for us?

Yes. Request a demo or message us on WhatsApp.
