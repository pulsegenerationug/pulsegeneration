PulseRemit Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 (version 1.3)

PulseRemit Pro is business software developed by Pulse Generation UG ("we", "us") for banks, money-transfer operators, microfinance institutions, SACCOs and similar organisations ("the institution"). It is used by the institution's staff, not by the general public.

1. Who controls the data

All information recorded in PulseRemit Pro is entered by the institution's staff and belongs to the institution. The institution is the data controller and is responsible for its lawful use under the laws of its country. Pulse Generation UG operates the "Pulse cloud" only as a processor that connects the institution's own computers (section 3). We never sell this data or use it for advertising.

2. Information the app stores

To run branch operations the app stores, on the institution's own devices:

  • customer details entered by staff: names, phone numbers, addresses, date of birth, nationality, next of kin, identification document details, photographs and specimen signatures;
  • financial records: transfer instructions, payouts, savings/deposit account transactions, cash sessions, expenses and fees;
  • staff user accounts (passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes), device registrations and an audit log of actions.

3. Where it is stored and sent

  • Data is stored in a database on the institution's Branch Host computer or standalone device.
  • Counter PCs and tablets connect to the Branch Host over the institution's local network with the Host's PIN; each device gets its own key, every request is encrypted (AES-256-GCM), and managers can block any device.
  • When a computer is online, the Pulse cloud (a Supabase database operated by Pulse Generation UG) connects the institution's head office and branches. It receives, over HTTPS: transfers sent between branches (sender and receiver names and phone numbers, amounts, status), the branch list, daily totals and the institution's settings (fees, exchange rates, products, logo).
  • For banking and SACCO services the cloud also receives, so that any branch of the same institution can serve its customers: a balance snapshot of each savings/deposit account (account number, holder name and number, product, currency, balance, status and only the last 4 digits of the phone number), a loan snapshot (loan number, borrower name, amounts outstanding and in arrears, days late, next due date) and a register of transaction receipts (receipt number, type, amount, account or loan number, branch, date) used to check that a printed receipt is genuine.
  • If the institution chooses Back up to cloud, a full backup is uploaded after it has been encrypted on the institution's computer (AES-256-GCM with a key derived from a password only the institution knows). Pulse Generation UG and Supabase cannot read it. The newest 3 backups per computer are kept.
  • Customer photos, signatures and identity documents are not sent to the cloud except inside such an encrypted backup; they stay on the branch computer.
  • Each institution's computers hold their own secret keys and the cloud only returns that institution's own records — one institution can never see another's data. Pulse Generation UG staff do not browse institution records.
  • For licensing and support we also receive: the institution's name and contacts, its computers' Device IDs, app versions, licence status and any licence/renewal request it sends.
  • Internal messages that staff send to the head office or to other branches of the same institution (sender name and role, recipient, subject, text, time) pass through the Pulse cloud so they can be delivered. Messages between staff of one branch stay on that branch's network. Only the institution's own computers can read its messages; Pulse Generation UG sees only how many messages were sent, never their content.
  • Messages the institution sends to Pulse Generation UG from Messages › Pulse Generation UG (support chat) are stored in the cloud so that we can reply.
  • The institution can switch cloud sync off in Settings › Head office & cloud. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Permissions

  • Camera - to photograph customers and receivers for identity verification, only when staff tap "Take photo".
  • Network access - to connect to the Branch Host on the local network and to the Pulse cloud.

5. Security

Role-based permissions, manager approval of devices, dual approval of large transactions, automatic sign-out, lock-out after failed sign-ins, and a tamper-evident audit log protect the data. Financial records are never deleted; corrections are recorded as new entries.

6. Retention and deletion

The institution decides how long records are kept, subject to the financial-record rules of its country. Staff accounts are disabled by the institution's administrator. Customers who want to access or correct their information should contact the institution that serves them.

7. Children

PulseRemit Pro is a business tool for adult staff and is not directed at children.
