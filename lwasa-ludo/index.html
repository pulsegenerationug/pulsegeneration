Game modes

Six ways to play.
No internet needed.

vs Computer

Six AI levels, from Beginner to Master. Stronger players hunt captures, guard their pawns and play the odds, but they always get the same fair dice as you.

Pass & Play

Two to four friends share one phone or PC. A quick banner shows whose turn it is, so the game never stops. Mix humans and computer players any way you like.

Blitz New

Everyone starts with a pawn already out and the clock is ticking: 3, 5 or 8 minutes. When time runs out, the furthest pawns win.

Team Battle, Quick & Custom

Blue + Green against Red + Yellow, a fast two-pawn game, or your own match built from eight rule switches.

The experience

Say goodbye to flat boards.

3D dice & effects

Every player has their own 3D dice, plus capture bursts, home sparkles, confetti and reactions from the computer players.

Luganda commentary

Voice cues that react to the game, from “Tugende!” and “Mukaga!” to “Kuba!” and “Omuwanguzi!”.

Save & resume

Every match saves itself at every turn. Close the app and pick up exactly where you left off.

Hints & move previews

Rings show where each pawn would land, and a red target marks a capture. Stuck? Tap the light bulb for a hint.

Trophies & stats

16 trophies, levels and XP, win streaks, your fastest win, wins against each AI level and your last 30 matches.

Play anywhere

Android phone on the go or Windows PC at home, with keyboard shortcuts on desktop (Space to roll, H for a hint).

The Locker

Earn it. Style it.

Win matches, claim the daily reward and unlock trophies to earn coins, then spend them on 10 dice skins and 6 board themes. Coins can only be earned by playing. Nothing is ever sold for money.
