Game modes

Play your way.
With anyone, anywhere.

vs Computer

Three AI levels. Easy plays casually, Medium hunts captures and safe squares, and Hard blocks, sacrifices and plays the odds. Works completely offline.

Pass & Play

Up to four players on one phone or PC. Perfect for family evenings, with no internet needed.

Online multiplayer

Real-time matches against players everywhere. Your moves register instantly.

Private rooms

The experience

Say goodbye to flat boards.

Immersive 3D

High-fidelity 3D dice and tokens, smooth animations and striking effects for every capture and victory.

Luganda commentary

Custom audio commentary and sound effects that react to the game, from “Mukaga!” to “Tugende!”.

Play anywhere

Your Windows desktop at home or your Android phone on the go. Same game, same feel.

Leaderboard

Climb the rankings against friends and players everywhere, with your games played and wins on your profile.

Free coins

Earn in-game coins simply by playing. No real money is needed to enjoy the game.

Premium, iOS-style design

Frosted-glass panels, fluid motion and a calm, clear interface that keeps the board in focus.

Quick chat

Fair play & privacy

Skill and luck. Nothing else.

Fair matches

Every player gets the same rules and the same random dice. Skill and a little luck decide the winner.

No pay-to-win

Nothing you can buy gives you an advantage at the table.

Offline collects nothing

vs Computer and Pass & Play send no data at all. Online play uses only your email, display name, stats and preset chat. Read the privacy policy.
