Lwasa Ludo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Lwasa Ludo is developed by Pulse Generation UG ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it. Lwasa Ludo is a Ludo board game and does not require an account to play offline.

1. Information we collect

Playing offline (Pass & Play or vs. Computer) collects nothing at all - no account, no data leaves your device.

If you choose to play online, we collect only what is needed to run a multiplayer match and a leaderboard:

  • Email address - used solely to sign you in and recover your account. Never shown to other players.
  • Display name - the name other players see at the table.
  • Game statistics - games played, wins, and the free in-game coins balance earned by playing.
  • In-game chat - when you use the chat feature, the preset message or emote you send is stored with the room so other players in that room can see it. There is no free-text chat.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and the app shows no advertising.

2. How we use your information

Solely to operate the game: matching you into a room, keeping score, running the leaderboard, and remembering your earned coins and cosmetic items between sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Where your information is stored

Online data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to your account data is restricted to you; other players can only ever see your display name, win/loss record, and anything you send in the in-game chat.

4. Payments

Lwasa Ludo is completely free. There are no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions anywhere in the app - the in-game shop only accepts coins earned by playing.

5. Children's privacy

Lwasa Ludo is a general-audience board game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the display name and email needed for the optional online account, which we recommend a parent set up on a child's behalf where required by local law.

6. Your choices

  • You can play the entire game offline without ever creating an account.
  • You can change your display name at any time from Settings.
  • You can request deletion of your online account and all associated data (email, display name, stats, coins, and chat history) at any time by contacting us using the details below - we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
