Lwasa Ludo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Lwasa Ludo is developed by Pulse Generation UG ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app collects. The short version: none.

1. Information we collect

Lwasa Ludo is a fully offline game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no online multiplayer, no advertising and no analytics. It does not request the internet permission, and no information about you or your games is sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the game keeps a few things in the app's private storage on your own device only:

  • your chosen player name and avatar,
  • your settings (sound, timer, computer level, chosen dice and board),
  • your statistics, match history, trophies, coins and level,
  • a saved match, if you choose to continue one later.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Payments

Lwasa Ludo is completely free. There are no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions. In-game coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.

4. Links

The About screen contains links to our website and social media pages. Tapping one opens it in another app (for example your browser), which has its own privacy policy.

5. Children's privacy

Lwasa Ludo is a general-audience board game. Because it collects no personal information at all, it is suitable for players of all ages.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
