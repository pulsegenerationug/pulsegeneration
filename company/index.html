Company

Software that keeps working, wherever you are.

Pulse Generation UG is a Ugandan software company. We build offline-first apps for hospitals, laboratories, schools, banks and SACCOs, pharmacies and shops, and landlords, plus a game for everyone.

Lwasa GeorgeFounder & Software Architect
Our story

Built for the conditions we really face.

Most business software assumes steady power and fast internet. Across East Africa, neither is guaranteed. So we build apps that run on your own computers and phones, share data over your own network, and keep working when the power or the connection drops.

Pulse Generation UG is led by Lwasa George, who makes every technical decision across our products, from the architecture to the screens people use every day.

Software should keep working when the power drops or the internet doesn’t cooperate, because that’s the reality of the places it’s built for.

How we work

Offline-first

Local operation is a design decision, not a fallback. The internet adds features; it never blocks work.

Reliability

Built to keep working under real conditions: power cuts, old computers, busy days. See how we protect your data.

Local context

Mobile Money, local currencies, Luganda and 13 interface languages in our health apps, and the workflows of real East African organisations.

Always improving

Regular free updates, listed in our changelog, shaped by what customers ask for.

People you can reach

Support on WhatsApp, phone and e-mail from the team that builds the apps.

Every platform

Android, Windows and Mac, from one phone at a reception desk to many computers across branches.
