MotoPulse - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 (version 1.0.0)

MotoPulse is business software developed by Pulse Generation UG ("we", "us") for car washes, boda wash points, parkings, detailing studios and garages (the "business"). It is used by the business's staff, not by the general public.

1. Who controls the data

All information recorded in MotoPulse is entered by the business's staff and belongs to the business. The business is the data controller and is responsible for its lawful use under the laws of its country (in Uganda, the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019). Pulse Generation UG operates the "Pulse cloud" only as a processor that connects the business's own computers (section 3). We never sell this data or use it for advertising.

2. Information the app stores

To run daily operations the app stores, on the business's own devices:

  • customers: name, phone numbers, area, type of account, daily amount, credit limit, notes and, only with the customer's agreement, a photo;
  • vehicles: number plate, type, make, model, colour and an optional photo;
  • jobs (washing, detailing, repairs), the condition of the vehicle at check-in, parking tickets, daily-customer arrivals, bills, payments (method and transaction reference), promises to pay and reminders;
  • employees: name, job title, phone, pay basis, an optional photo for staff ID cards, attendance and the work each person recorded or did;
  • staff user accounts (passwords only as salted one-way hashes), device registrations and an audit log of actions.

3. Where it is stored and sent

  • Data is stored in a database on the business's main (Host) computer or standalone device.
  • Other computers, phones and tablets connect to the Host over the business's local network after pairing with the Host's PIN; every request is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and managers can block any device.
  • When a computer is online, the Pulse cloud (a Supabase database operated by Pulse Generation UG) connects the business's head office and branches. Over HTTPS it receives:
    • daily totals for the head office: money received today and this month, amount billed, number of jobs and parking stays, vehicles parked now, total owed by customers, number of customers and employees;
    • from the head office to its branches: service names and prices per vehicle type, parking rates and the business's settings;
    • internal messages that staff send to the head office or other branches of the same business, so they can be delivered;
    • for licensing and support: the business's name and contacts, its computers' Device IDs, app versions, staff user names and roles, licence status, licence requests and the support messages it sends to us.
  • Customer, vehicle, number-plate, photo, job, parking and payment records stay on the business's computers.
  • The cloud only returns a business's own records to its own computers; one business can never see another's data. Pulse Generation UG staff do not browse business records.
  • The business can switch the cloud off in Settings. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Permissions

  • Camera - to take customer, employee and vehicle photos and scan QR codes, only when staff tap the camera button.
  • Face ID / fingerprint - optional quick sign-in and to confirm approvals; biometric data never leaves the device.
  • Bluetooth - to print receipts, job cards and parking tickets on a Bluetooth thermal printer chosen by the user.
  • Local network - to connect phones and tablets at the gate and the counter to the business's Host computer.
  • Network access - to connect to the Host computer and to the Pulse cloud.

5. Security

Role-based permissions, manager approval of devices, approvals for sensitive actions (voids, write-offs, credit above a limit), automatic sign-out, lock-out after failed sign-ins and a tamper-evident audit log protect the data. Payments and bills are never deleted, only voided with a reason.

6. Retention and deletion

Delete your account inside the app. Open Settings → Privacy & account → Delete my account and confirm with your password. Your name, phone, e-mail and sign-in are erased at once and you are signed out. Records a staff member entered (jobs, parking, receipts, payments) stay with the business.

Delete all of the business's data. The owner can open Settings → Privacy & account → Delete the business's data. Every record on that device is erased, and we are asked to delete the business's records from the Pulse cloud; we do this within 30 days.

Without the app. Write to pulsegeneration@gmail.com or use pulsegenerationug.com/delete-account. We confirm who you are, then complete the request within 30 days.

MotoPulse runs on Windows, Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac. The same rules apply on every device. On iPhone, iPad and Mac the app has no in-app purchases; licences are arranged directly with Pulse Generation UG.

7. Children

MotoPulse is a business tool for adult staff and is not directed at children.
