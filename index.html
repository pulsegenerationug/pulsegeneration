🏥 Hospital Management

PulseHMIS

Every department a patient passes through, connected — running entirely on your clinic's own local network. No internet outage has ever stopped a consultation.

01
Reception

Registration, visit creation, duplicate-patient detection.

02
Doctor's Office

Vitals with automatic BMI, multi-diagnosis, prescriptions.

03
Laboratory

Test ordering, structured results, verify & release.

04
Cashier

Flexible billing — cash, insurance, credit, installments.

05
Pharmacy

Dispensing against the exact prescription written.

06
Injection Room

Shift-based dose administration and history.

Full patient flow

Reception → Doctor's Office → Laboratory → Cashier → Pharmacy → Injection Room, all connected to one visit record.

Structured vitals

Temperature, pulse, BP, weight, and height feed straight into an automatic BMI calculation.

Multi-diagnosis & prescriptions

Primary and secondary diagnoses, with detailed, trackable prescription and continuation-medicine records.

Flexible billing

Cash, insurance, or credit — with full support for partial payments and installments over time.

Host + Client, no internet

One computer hosts the facility's data; every other computer connects over the local network. Nothing depends on a connection outside the building.

Branded documents

Your facility's own letterhead on every printed prescription, lab result, and receipt.

💰 Financial Auditing

FixIt Enterprise

Expense management and financial auditing built for businesses operating across borders and currencies.

Real-time balance analytics

Profit and deficit visibility the moment a transaction is entered — not at end of month.

One-tap vector PDF reports

Dual-matrix layout reporting, generated instantly and ready to share or print.

150+ ISO currency wheel

Built for cross-border operations, with fast switching between currencies as you work.

Multi-cloud sync

Data mirrored across Firebase and Supabase, so a single provider outage never puts your records at risk.

Native AirPrint

Print directly from the app to any AirPrint-compatible printer — no extra software needed.

Bank-level encryption

Row-Level Security and encrypted storage protect every record, on every device.
