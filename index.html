About Pulse Generation UG

Building software end-to-end, for the conditions East African businesses actually operate in.

Pulse Generation UG is directed by Lwasa George, who leads all technical decisions across the company's products — from architecture to the interfaces people use every day.

The company's philosophy is simple: reliability and practicality first. Software should keep working when power drops or the internet doesn't cooperate, because that's the reality of the environments it's built for.
