Help

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Send us a message and we’ll reply.

InstallingDo I need an account to download?

No. Every download is free and needs no account. Just pick your app and platform in Downloads.

InstallingAndroid says the app is from an unknown source. Is that normal?

Yes. Android shows this for any app installed outside the Play Store. Allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager when asked, then tap Install. Our APKs come straight from our official GitHub release.

InstallingWindows shows a SmartScreen warning. What should I do?

SmartScreen can warn about installers from newer publishers. Choose More info, then Run anyway, and follow the setup steps. The app then appears in your Start menu and on the desktop.

InstallingCan I use the apps on iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Today our apps run on Android phones and tablets and on Windows PCs. iOS and macOS versions are on the roadmap, and the apps are built with Flutter so they can follow without a rewrite.

PulseHMISDoes PulseHMIS need an internet connection?

No. Every clinical workflow (registration, consultation, lab, cashier, pharmacy and injection room) works fully offline over your facility’s own local network. The only thing that ever uses the internet is the subscription check, and patient care is never blocked by it.

PulseHMISHow do the different rooms stay in sync?

One computer runs as the Host; every other computer or tablet runs as a Client on the same WiFi or wired network. Changes are saved locally first, then synced to the Host in the background, using revision numbers rather than clocks, so nothing is lost if devices disagree on the time. See how it works.

PulseHMISHow much does PulseHMIS cost?

Installing is free, with a full trial of 5 days or 10 patient registrations, whichever comes first. After that it is 20,000 UGX per month per facility, with discounts for 6-month and yearly terms. If a subscription lapses, only administrative features pause. Clinical care never does. See pricing.

PulseHMISIs patient information protected?

Yes. Each staff role (Reception, Doctor, Nurse, Lab, Cashier, Pharmacy, Administrator) sees only what it needs. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, every action is recorded in the visit’s audit history, and no clinical data ever leaves your network.

EduPulseWhat kinds of institutions can use EduPulse?

Primary and secondary schools, colleges, universities, and vocational and training institutions, both private and government. Terms or semesters, grading scales and currency are all configurable, so nothing is hard-coded to one system.

EduPulseCan EduPulse print report cards with our school’s logo?

Yes. Set up your letterhead once: logos on the left, right or both sides, motto, address and contact details. Every report card, receipt, payslip and report uses it automatically, with signatures and your school stamp where you enable them.

EduPulseDoes EduPulse work without internet?

Yes. Registering students, attendance, marks, fees, receipts, report cards, payroll, backups and printing all work offline, stored safely in a local database on your device.

LudoCan I play Lwasa Ludo offline?

Yes. Play against the computer, or pass-and-play with up to four people on one device. It is fully offline: no account, no internet connection and no data collected.

LudoWhat does Lwasa Ludo collect?

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the app has no internet permission. Your progress, coins and trophies stay on your device. Read the full Lwasa Ludo privacy policy.

SupportCan you help us install and train our staff?

Yes. We can walk you through installation, set up the Host and Client computers, and train your team. Request a demo or message us on WhatsApp.
