PulseHMIS: getting started

Built with care, described honestly.

What the website does with your information, what PulseHMIS does, and who controls what.

This website

It stores only what you give it: messages you send and, if you register, your account details. It never handles patient or financial data.

PulseHMIS

Role-based access control and salted authentication, running offline-first on your local network by design. Patient records stay inside your building.

Your data

Messages and requests are used only to reply to you. You can ask for your data or account to be deleted at any time.

Software built end to end, for the conditions businesses here really face.

Pulse Generation UG is directed by Lwasa George, who leads every technical decision across the company’s products, from architecture to the interfaces people use every day.

The philosophy is simple: reliability and practicality first. Software should keep working when power drops or the internet doesn’t cooperate, because that’s the reality of the places it’s built for.
